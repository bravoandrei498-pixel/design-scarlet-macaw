## Why these choices aren't just taste

*(Optional talking point — useful when a client asks "why does this cost what it costs"
or pushes a preference that fights the design.)*

Plain English, if it comes up:

- **Good-looking builds trust.** People consistently rate better-looking sites as easier
  to use — and judge the business behind them as more capable. Looking polished isn't
  vanity; it changes whether someone calls you.
- **Familiar beats clever.** Your visitors spend their day on other websites, so yours
  should work the way those do. We put the personality in how it *looks*, not in making
  people relearn how a website behaves.
- **Fewer choices, more action.** Every extra button or field slows a decision down.
  That's why each page pushes one clear next step rather than five.

## The 4th option — Mix & Match

You're not locked into picking exactly one. You can combine elements — e.g. "the colors
from B with the layout feel of A." If you want to mix, tell us which pieces from which
direction, and we'll restate the combined choices before building so everyone's aligned
on exactly what's being built. (Some combinations need a small amount of reconciliation
work — we'll flag it plainly if so, per `02-MASTER-PLAYBOOK.md` Rule 1c.)

---

## What happens after you pick

1. We build the Brand Style Guide and full navigation first.
2. Then we build every page from your brief, in the direction (or mix) you chose.
3. You'll see the personalized version with your real logo, photos, and copy swapped
   in before final review — the direction is locked and beautiful before that happens,
   so changes at that stage are typically small (copy tweaks, image swaps).
