### Applies to: Residential Sales, Property Development, Renovation / Remodeling, Property Management, Commercial Real Estate

## PALETTE GUIDANCE

Real estate sites must convey aspiration, quality, and local expertise.

**Residential sales (premium/luxury):**
- Deep navy or charcoal + warm gold/champagne accent
- Cream and warm white backgrounds
- Feel: Aspirational, established, premium

**Residential sales (approachable/community-focused):**
- Warm navy or slate + soft terracotta or sage accent
- Off-white and warm neutral backgrounds
- Feel: Trustworthy, local, human

**Property development:**
- Bolder and more architectural — dark charcoal, concrete gray, deep slate
- Sharp contrast, minimal palette
- Accent: bronze, gold, or architectural green
- Feel: Visionary, sophisticated, confident

**Renovation / remodeling:**
- Warm and craft-forward — deep warm gray, warm white, natural wood tones
- Accent: terracotta, warm amber, sage
- Feel: Skilled craftsmanship, transformation, trust

**Avoid:**
- Bright, high-energy colors (reads as cheap listings, not premium service)
- Pure white only (flat and generic for a visual industry)
- Mismatched accent colors that fight each other

## TYPOGRAPHY GUIDANCE

**Recommended archetypes:**

**Premium Professional** (most residential real estate, luxury):
- Display: Clean, confident sans (DM Sans ExtraBold, Satoshi, Plus Jakarta Sans)
- Body: Albert Sans, DM Sans
- Feel: Modern, premium, trustworthy

**Warm Authority** (community-focused agents, long-established firms):
- Display: Elegant serif (Playfair Display, Lora)
- Body: Figtree, Source Sans 3
- Feel: Established, local knowledge, warm

**Bold Modern** (development, investment, commercial):
- Display: Grotesk heavy (Syne ExtraBold, Clash Display, Space Grotesk Bold)
- Body: Manrope, Outfit
- Feel: Visionary, forward-thinking, confident

## COPY TONE

Real estate copy must balance aspiration with local credibility.

**Do:**
- Lead with the client's goal: "Ready to find your forever home?" / "Time to sell for what your home is worth."
- Be specific about local market knowledge: neighborhoods, average prices, days on market
- Lead with outcomes: "Homes sold above asking price" / "30+ years finding families their perfect home"
- Use transformation language for renovation: "See what's possible"
- Make the process feel simple — buyers and sellers are often anxious about the process

**Don't:**
- Use vague luxury language ("dream home," "living your best life") without specifics
- Lead with agent credentials before establishing what you do for the client
- Use overly corporate/transactional language — this is about people's homes
- Overpromise on market outcomes

## REAL ESTATE-SPECIFIC TRUST SIGNALS

Priority order:

1. **Sales volume / homes sold** — "127 homes sold in 2024," "$42M in transactions"
2. **Days on market** — "Average 18 days on market" (if strong)
3. **List-to-sale ratio** — "Sellers receive 103% of list price on average"
4. **Years in the market / local expertise** — "15 years serving [City]"
5. **Client testimonials** — specific outcomes ("listed Monday, 3 offers by Wednesday")
6. **Industry recognition** — Top Producer, RE/MAX Diamond, local association awards
7. **Named agents with credentials** — licensed, years of experience, specialization
8. **Local market data** — demonstrates expertise and authority

## HOMEPAGE SECTION ORDER (Real Estate)

1. Announcement bar (free valuation / consultation + phone)
2. Hero (aspirational headline, dual CTA, trust micro-element)
3. Trust/Stats bar (homes sold, sales volume, years, rating)
4. Brand story / local expertise positioning
5. Services preview (Buy / Sell / Invest, or service categories for renovation)
6. Featured portfolio / recent projects or listings
7. Market stats / local knowledge section
8. Testimonials (transaction-specific quotes)
9. Meet the Agents / Team
10. Service areas (neighborhoods, cities served)
11. CTA conversion band
12. FAQ + free valuation / consultation form (PageOutro)
13. Footer

## PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION

- **Architectural and lifestyle photography is the product** — invest accordingly
- Wide-angle interior shots: bright, airy, aspirational
- Exterior shots: golden hour, clean sky, front-facing
- Lifestyle inserts: family in kitchen, morning coffee on patio — not staged or forced
- For renovation: before/after is more powerful than after-only
- Never: dark, low-resolution, fisheye-distorted interior shots
- Never: generic stock (couple shaking hands with agent, keys in hand, etc.)
- Agent headshots: outdoors or in-home setting, approachable and professional
